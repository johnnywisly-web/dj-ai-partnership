Purpose
DJ AI Partnership LLC and JL Fitness are exploring a potential collaboration concerning the responsible collection, preparation, anonymization, and potential licensing of real-world operational data for AI training and technical evaluation.

Any data considered for sharing must be handled under mutually agreed confidentiality and security requirements. Personal, sensitive, or otherwise restricted information should be removed, anonymized, or redacted as appropriate before authorized transfer.

Commercial terms
The parties may negotiate data access, licensing, compensation, permitted uses, security requirements, delivery methods, retention, and other commercial terms.

Important status
This document describes a proposed collaboration framework for discussion. It is not, by itself, a final NDA, data license, purchase agreement, or transfer of ownership. Any binding arrangement should be documented and signed by authorized representatives of the parties.
